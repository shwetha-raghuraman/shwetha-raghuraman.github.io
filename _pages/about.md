I am an applied microeconomist studying education and inequality. My research spans topics such as school choice, teacher impacts on students, free tuition program design, and higher education finance. I use administrative data and apply experimental, quasi-experimental, and structural methods in my research.

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Economics and Business Economics at Aarhus University and an Affiliate Scholar at Oberlin College. I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Michigan in 2024.
